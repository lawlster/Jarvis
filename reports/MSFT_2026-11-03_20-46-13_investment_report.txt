## **1. COMPANY OVERVIEW**  
**Industry & Core Business:**  
Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) operates in the **Technology** sector, specifically within the **Software - Infrastructure** industry. The company generates revenue through a diversified portfolio of products and services, including:  
- **Cloud computing (Azure):** A leading global cloud infrastructure and platform services provider.  
- **Productivity software (Office 365, Teams):** Subscription-based tools for businesses and consumers.  
- **Enterprise services (Windows, SQL Server, Dynamics 365):** Licensing and cloud-based solutions.  
- **AI and data analytics:** Emerging revenue streams through Azure AI and cloud-based analytics tools.  

**Revenue Model:**  
Microsoft’s revenue model is primarily **subscription-based** (recurring revenue from cloud services) and **licensing** (one-time purchases for software). The company emphasizes **recurring revenue** and **operating leverage**, which drive long-term profitability.  

**Key Differentiators:**  
- **Ecosystem integration:** Seamless connectivity between Windows, Office, Azure, and other Microsoft platforms.  
- **R&D investment:** Heavy focus on AI, quantum computing, and cloud innovation.
